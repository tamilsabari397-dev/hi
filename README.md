# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple interest calculator finds the interest earned or paid on a principal amount over a period of time, without adding previously earned interest to the principal.

## Formula

```text
Simple Interest = (Principal x Rate x Time) / 100
Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest
```

- **Principal**: The starting amount of money.
- **Rate**: The annual interest rate, expressed as a percentage.
- **Time**: The duration in years.

Use the same currency for the principal and the resulting amounts. If the duration is given in months, convert it to years by dividing by 12.

## Example

For a principal of 1,000 at an annual rate of 5% for 2 years:

```text
Simple Interest = (1000 x 5 x 2) / 100 = 100
Total Amount = 1000 + 100 = 1100
```

## Calculator Steps

1. Enter the principal, annual rate, and time in years.
2. Calculate the simple interest using the formula above.
3. Add the interest to the principal to get the total amount.

This calculator describes simple interest only; it does not account for compounding, fees, taxes, or changing rates.
